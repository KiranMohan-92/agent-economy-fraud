\section{Research Score and Validation Protocol}\label{sec:detection}

The implementation computes descriptive transaction features.  It does not
observe controller intent, authorization, deception, victim loss, criminal
proceeds, or market manipulation.  We therefore call its output a
\emph{registry-proxy research score}, not a fraud probability or risk decision.
The only evaluated question is whether a score ranks historical ERC-8004
registry owner/default-wallet proxies above active counterparties of unknown
type in one Base-chain snapshot.

\subsection{Observed and latent variables}

Let $X_a$ be transaction-derived features for address $a$, $R_a$ indicate the
historical registry proxy, $A_a$ denote verified autonomous execution, and
$F_a$ denote an adjudicated financial-crime outcome.  The retained data contain
$X_a$ and a proxy for $R_a$; they do not contain $A_a$ or $F_a$.  Thus the
estimable quantity is a ranking functional such as
\begin{equation}
  \Pr\{S(X_{R=1}) > S(X_{R=0})\},
\end{equation}
where $R=0$ means ``active counterparty not in the selected registry set,'' not
``human'' or ``legitimate.''  No transformation of this experiment identifies
$\Pr(F=1\mid X)$.

\subsection{Candidate transaction features}

The code exposes four active feature families and one inactive placeholder.
They are common graph and behavioral descriptors, not properties that agents
``cannot avoid.''  Each admits legitimate and malicious counterexamples.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Implemented candidate features and their observation boundary.}
\label{tab:candidate_features}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Feature} & \textbf{Observed pattern} & \textbf{Non-claim / benign twin} \\
\midrule
Economic rationality (ER) & amount, concentration, circular-flow heuristics
  & Small payments, liquidity routing, and circular flows are not fraud labels. \\
Network topology (NT) & address degree, counterparty concentration, graph paths
  & Exchanges, routers, payroll, and campaigns can be hubs. \\
Value flow (VF) & rapid forwarding and fan-out heuristics
  & Treasury, bridge, and payment-processor flows can look similar. \\
Temporal consistency (TC) & cadence, bursts, and inter-event variation
  & Scheduled services are legitimate; stochastic attackers can imitate a target cadence. \\
Cross-platform (CP) & intended cross-chain correlation
  & Inactive in the single-chain experiment and assigned weight zero. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The feature modules return address-level values in $[0,1]$.  Their internal
heuristics should be treated as hypotheses whose direction, time window, and
benign twins must be preregistered before a confirmatory test.  Transaction
graphs must be constructed independently inside every training split; otherwise
held-out addresses can leak through shared neighbors and edges.

\subsection{Untuned baseline and exploratory fit}\label{subsec:fusion}

The corrected implementation defaults to a deliberately untuned average over
the four active features,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:baseline_score}
  S_0(a)=0.25\,ER_a+0.25\,NT_a+0.25\,VF_a+0.25\,TC_a,
\end{equation}
with $CP$ excluded.  $S_0$ is a reference calculation, not a probability.
The implementation exposes no \textsc{allow}, \textsc{investigate}, or
\textsc{block} tier.

For forensic comparison only, a post-hoc rule fitted to the retained labels
assigns
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exploratory_score}
  S_{\rm exp}(a)=0.1116\,ER_a+0.7269\,NT_a+0.1615\,VF_a.
\end{equation}
These weights are proportional to positive full-sample values of
$\auc_i-0.5$.  Because the same population selected and evaluated the rule,
$S_{\rm exp}$ is explicitly non-confirmatory.  A below-chance feature cannot
be silently zeroed, inverted, or reinterpreted after looking at the test set in
a confirmatory study; its treatment must be fixed using training data alone.

The historical threshold $0.09$ remains only as a named reference for
reconstructing old reports.  It is not optimal under the new baseline, does not
control a verified false-positive rate, and must not cause an adverse action.

\subsection{Four separable research tasks}

The original project collapsed four different targets.  A valid program keeps
them separate:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \textbf{Registry or automation attribution:} identify verified
    controller/executor classes, including humans, services, contracts,
    exchanges, generic automation, verified autonomous agents, and unknowns.
  \item \textbf{Control-plane security:} detect mandate escalation, prompt or
    tool injection, provenance breaks, policy bypass, and key compromise using
    runtime and wallet-policy telemetry.
  \item \textbf{Fraud and scam detection:} predict independently adjudicated
    unauthorized or deceptive loss with customer, beneficiary, case, and
    intervention outcomes.
  \item \textbf{AML and market-abuse surveillance:} use distinct labels,
    legal hypotheses, entity resolution, graph windows, trade/order-book data,
    beneficial ownership, and investigator outcomes.
\end{enumerate}

Success in task 1 neither proves nor is a logical prerequisite for task 3.
Actor-agnostic controls can detect an unauthorized transfer without proving
whether its executor was a person, script, or language-model agent.

\subsection{Confirmatory evaluation contract}

A future test must freeze, before examining its final holdout: the target
construct; unit of analysis; registry, autonomy, authorization, and outcome
evidence; inclusion window; feature definitions; graph construction; missing
data policy; model family; calibration; threshold; investigator capacity;
metrics; benign twins; and kill criteria.  Cohort construction, cleaning,
feature extraction, weight fitting, calibration, and threshold selection must
occur inside an outer temporal or connected-component split.  The untouched
test should report PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, calibration error, precision and recall at
capacity, component-aware uncertainty, incremental lift over established
controls, and intervention benefit.

\subsection{Operational safety gate}

Until that contract is satisfied, the score is restricted to offline research
and, at most, monitored shadow analysis.  It must not be used as a sole input
to payment blocking, account restriction, customer accusation, suspicious
activity reporting, or law-enforcement referral.  Production consideration
requires adjudicated outcomes, documented data lineage, model-risk review,
privacy and fairness assessment, alert-capacity testing, rollback controls, and
evidence that intervention reduces harm relative to existing controls.
