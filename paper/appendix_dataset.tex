\section{Evidence Vector and Dataset Contract}\label{app:dataset}

\subsection{Independent evidence dimensions}

Evidence dimensions are non-monotonic.  Each address, event, or case receives a
time-stamped vector whose components can be present, absent, or unknown:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}c l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Evidence dimension} & \textbf{Example use} \\
\midrule
$R$ & registry record & registry association \\
$W$ & current verified wallet association & current wallet-control association \\
$S$ & live service & operational-service observation \\
$A$ & authenticated autonomous-execution trace & bounded execution attribution \\
$C$ & commerce/payment instruction and settlement chain & delegated-payment observation \\
$P$ & independently verified person/human control & verified-human comparison \\
$Y$ & independently adjudicated task outcome & task-specific fraud, AML, or market-abuse evaluation \\
$H$ & measured harm or intervention outcome & loss or control-effectiveness evaluation \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Claims name their own required conjunction: $R$ alone supports registry
association; $A$ (with time-correct address or wallet linkage) supports observed
autonomous execution, with $W$ or $S$ added only when the scoped mechanism
depends on them; $C$ is additionally required for a delegated-payment claim;
$P$ is required for a verified-human comparator; $Y$ is required for task
performance; and $Y+H$ is required for effectiveness or harm-reduction claims.
No component implies another. The Stage~2 snapshot supplies only $R$-loader
evidence (selection through the loader's owner or mint-recipient relation), not
transaction-time $R$, and leaves $W,S,A,C,P,Y,H$ unknown. The injection experiment is a code-level
synthetic response test, not evidence for any of those real-world dimensions.

\subsection{Minimum release contract}

An admissible replacement corpus must publish or escrow: chain and contract;
exact versioned SQL/query identifier; inclusive start and exclusive end block
or timestamp; extraction time; event identity $(\texttt{chain},
\texttt{tx\_hash},\texttt{evt\_index})$; raw and derived schemas; address and
entity inclusion flow; registration and wallet-state timestamps; label-source
and confidence fields; unknown class; exclusions; license; file sizes; SHA-256
hashes; and environment/command provenance.  Dataset changes require a new
version and fresh confirmatory holdout.

The local historical artifacts are retained only for forensic audit.  They are
marked superseded in \texttt{data/MANIFEST.md}; they must not be presented as a
released benchmark or silently regenerated into the same version.
